Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 9, 2026

About the site

METAL HUB is a personal, non-profit project: a knowledge base and calculator collection for mechanical manufacturing engineering, in Hebrew and English. We believe everyone should be able to browse it equally, independently and comfortably, and we are continuously working to improve its accessibility.

Accessibility level

The site is in the process of being made accessible. It does not yet fully meet Israeli Standard SI 5568 (based on the WCAG guidelines, level AA), and we are working to move closer to it step by step.

What is already in place
  • An accessibility toolbar on every page (floating button at the bottom corner of the screen): larger/smaller text, high contrast, invert colors, grayscale, highlight links, readable font, stop animations, large cursor and a reading guide. Your settings are remembered in your browser.
  • The site is available in Hebrew (right-to-left) and English (left-to-right), with language and direction set on every page.
  • Light and dark display modes (the ☀️ button at the top of the page).
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes.
  • Background animations stop automatically for visitors whose operating system requests reduced motion.
Known limitations
  • Keyboard navigation: some interactive components (tabs, pop-up windows, search results) are not yet fully adapted.
  • Screen readers: in some calculators the labels are not programmatically linked to their fields, and dynamically updated results are not announced automatically.
  • Color contrast: some text (mainly gray secondary text) may not meet the required contrast ratio.
  • Videos: links to explanatory videos lead to YouTube search results. Their accessibility (captions, etc.) is the responsibility of the external site.
  • The AI assistant and calculators are dynamic components and may be difficult to use with some assistive technologies.
Contact us about accessibility

Ran into a difficulty, or have a suggestion? We would like to hear from you. Please include the page address, a description of the problem, and the browser or assistive technology you used. We will try to provide the information in an alternative way and to fix the problem as soon as possible.
