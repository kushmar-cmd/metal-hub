An animated course on welding: from the electric arc and the welding processes, through special and robotic welding, metallurgy, steels, aluminum and titanium and distortion, to welding symbols, weld calculation, defects, inspection, procedures and qualification. Each chapter explains how it works, what the parameters are, what goes wrong and what drives the cost.
