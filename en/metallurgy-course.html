Materials and Metallurgy

An animated video course: from the grain inside the metal to the decision of which material to order. Every chapter explains what happens in the material, how it affects work in the shop, and what drives the cost.

What happens in the material and why, the key numbers, real examples, what it means in the shop, and finally: what drives the cost.

Who the course is for

Engineers, designers, production and purchasing staff, technicians and operators who want to understand why a material behaves the way it does, and how to choose and order it correctly.
