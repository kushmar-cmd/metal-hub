💡 Flange tightening sequence: star/cross pattern in 3 stages (30% → 70% → 100%) + a final check pass. Sequential (circular) tightening is prohibited! The first bolt causes the part to distort. Gasket: re-torque after 24h hot (gasket creep). ▶ YouTube
📐 Preload — Tightening Theory
🎯 Why Preload?
• The bolt doesn't feel external loads as long as Preload > external load
• Without preload: every load cycle = a fatigue cycle on the bolt → fracture!
• With preload: the clamped parts absorb 90%+ of the load → the bolt stays "alive"
• Target: Preload = 70-90% of Sy × As
📊 The Problem: 90% Goes to Friction!
• ~50% of the torque → bolt head/nut friction
• ~40% → thread friction
• Only 10% → bolt tension (Preload)!
• Therefore: K-Factor (lubrication condition) controls everything
• Same torque, lubricated = 60% higher preload than dry!
🔧 Tightening Methods (precise → less)
1. Torque-Angle — Snug + angle = ±5% Preload ★★★★★
2. Yield-Point (TTY) — to yield = ±3% ★★★★★
3. Controlled Torque — calibrated wrench = ±25% ★★★☆☆
4. Turn-of-Nut — steel construction = ±15% ★★★★☆
5. Stretch — elongation measurement = ±1% ★★★★★
6. Ultrasonic — Bolt Gauge = ±1% ★★★★★
⚠️ Common Failures
• Insufficient Preload — the #1 cause of vibration loosening
• Over-Tightening — a 12.9 bolt + Anti-Seize = breakage!
• Unknown Friction — dry vs. lubricated = ±60% variance
• Wrong Sequence — sequential instead of star pattern = distortion
• Reusing TTY — a bolt that has yielded plastically = single-use!
