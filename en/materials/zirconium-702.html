Zirconium 702

Zirconium 702 is commercially pure zirconium for chemical equipment. It is highly resistant to hydrochloric and sulfuric acid (over many concentrations), organic acids and hot alkalis, so it is used where both stainless and titanium fail.

An important distinction: R60702 naturally contains hafnium, which absorbs neutrons. For nuclear use hafnium is removed (for example in R60001 sponge per ASTM B349), and Zircaloy alloys are used for reactor fuel cladding.

Fabrication

Safety: fire

Fine chips and dust of zirconium are highly flammable, like magnesium. Do not accumulate dust, and keep suitable extinguishing means.

Corrosion

Limits: hydrofluoric acid, and ferric or cupric ions in hydrochloric solutions.

Welding

By TIG with full gas shielding, like titanium.

Common mistakes

Using R60702 in a nuclear reactor

Its hafnium absorbs neutrons. For nuclear use, hafnium-free grades.

Accumulating chips and dust

Fire hazard.

Using it in hydrochloric solutions with ferric ions

The corrosion rate rises sharply.
