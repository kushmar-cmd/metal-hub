7175-T74 Aluminum

A 7000-series Al-Zn-Mg-Cu alloy, a refined version of 7075 for forgings

Typical values for the temper in the title

7175 has almost the same composition as 7075, but with lower iron and silicon limits. Fewer impurities mean fewer particles from which cracks start, so better toughness and fatigue resistance.

It is used mainly for aerospace forgings in overaged tempers (T74), where it combines good strength (about 490 MPa) with stress corrosion resistance.

Common tempers

Applications

  • Aerospace forgings
  • Forged structural parts
  • Fatigue-loaded parts
  • Parts where 7075 is not tough enough
  • Critical forgings

Fabrication

Machining

Machines well. The stress-relieved tempers (Tx51) allow a lot of material to be removed without significant distortion.

Welding

Fusion welding is not recommended. Friction stir welding (FSW) is possible.

Corrosion and protection

The overaged tempers improve resistance to stress corrosion cracking and exfoliation, but protection is still applied: conversion coating, anodizing and paint.

Common mistakes

Welding

7xxx alloys with copper are not suited to fusion welding. Join with rivets and bolts.

Substituting a temper without checking

The overaged tempers differ in strength and stress corrosion resistance. The temper on the drawing is binding.

Replacing 7175 with 7075 in a critical forging

The composition is similar, but the toughness differs. In a critical part, the substitution needs approval.
