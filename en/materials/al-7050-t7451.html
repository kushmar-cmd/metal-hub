7050-T7451 Aluminum

A 7000-series Al-Zn-Mg-Cu alloy, for thick aerospace parts

Typical values for the temper in the title

7050 was developed as an improvement on 7075 for thick sections. Instead of chromium it contains zirconium, which is less sensitive to the cooling rate in heat treatment. So even at the center of a thick plate it reaches high strength; in T7451 the tensile strength is 496–531 MPa.

In addition, its usual tempers (T74 and T76) are overaged, giving much better stress corrosion resistance and higher toughness than 7075-T6. It is the alloy of fuselage frames, bulkheads and wing panels.

Applications

  • Fuselage frames and bulkheads
  • Wing panels and primary structure
  • Parts machined from thick plate (50–150 mm)
  • Military aircraft structures
  • Aerospace forgings

Fabrication

Machining

Machines well, and the T7451 temper (stress relieved) allows large amounts of material to be removed from thick plate without significant distortion.

Welding

Fusion welding is not recommended: the alloy is prone to hot cracking, and the heat-affected zone weakens.

Corrosion and protection

More resistant to stress corrosion cracking than 7075-T6, but it still needs protection: conversion coating, anodizing and paint.

Common mistakes

Choosing 7075-T6 for thick plate

At the center of thick plate 7075 does not reach full strength, and T6 is susceptible to stress corrosion cracking. For thick plate, 7050-T7451.

Welding 7050

Fusion welding creates cracks and a weak zone. Join with bolts or rivets.

Ordering without a temper

T7451 and T7651 differ in corrosion resistance. The drawing should state the temper.
