CuCrZr (CW106C / C18150)

Age-hardened copper, for welding electrodes

Typical values for the temper in the title

CuCrZr is copper with about 1% chromium and a little zirconium, hardened by aging. It keeps high electrical and thermal conductivity (at least 75–80% IACS), but is much stronger and harder than pure copper, and keeps its hardness when hot.

This makes it the standard material for spot welding electrodes and caps (ISO 5182 Class A2/2, RWMA Class 2), seam welding wheels, electrode holders, and parts needing both conductivity and strength: contacts, heat spreaders and mold cooling inserts.

Tempers and heat treatment

CuCrZr gains strength by solution treatment and aging, sometimes with cold work between the steps.

ConditionPropertiesWhen to choose it
Solution treated and agedbalanced strength and conductivitymost uses

Applications

  • Spot welding electrodes and caps
  • Seam welding wheels
  • Electrode holders
  • Loaded electrical contacts
  • Mold cooling inserts

Fabrication

Machining

Moderate: copper "smears". Sharp tools with positive rake.

Welding

Do not fusion weld: heat cancels the aging and the material softens. Join mechanically or by soldering.

Choosing vs beryllium copper

CuCrZr is more conductive and cheaper, without beryllium dust risk. BeCu is stronger.

Common mistakes

Welding a CuCrZr part

It softens in the weld zone.

Using pure copper for an electrode

It deforms and wears quickly.

Heating above the aging temperature for long

The material softens.
