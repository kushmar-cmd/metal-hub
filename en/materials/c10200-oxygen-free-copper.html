Typical values for the temper in the title

C10200 is oxygen-free (OF) copper with at least 99.95% purity. Without oxygen, it does not embrittle when heated in hydrogen, unlike ETP copper (C11000), which contains oxides. Its electrical and thermal conductivity is excellent.

It is used for conductors and busbars, vacuum and accelerator components, RF waveguides, and parts brazed in hydrogen furnaces. For the highest purity (99.99%, OFE) there is C10100.

Fabrication

Machining

Difficult: pure copper "smears" and sticks to the tool. Very sharp tools, large positive rake.

Brazing and welding

Excellent, and no hydrogen embrittlement.

Vacuum

Low outgassing, so it suits high vacuum.

Common mistakes

Using C11000 in hydrogen brazing

It embrittles. Use oxygen-free copper.

Machining with ordinary tools

Copper sticks to the tool.

Choosing C10200 when a lot of machining is needed

C14500 machines much better.
