Hexagonal Boron Nitride (hBN)

A soft, machinable ceramic

1.9–2.1 g/cm³Density
14–60 GPaModulus E
90% — ordinary machiningMachinability

Typical values for the temper in the title

Hexagonal boron nitride is built in layers, like graphite, so it is soft (Mohs 2), slippery, and machinable with ordinary tools, unlike most ceramics. It is electrically insulating, thermal shock resistant, and not wetted by most molten metals and glasses.

It is used for high-temperature insulators and in vacuum furnaces, nozzles and molds for aluminum and molten metals, semiconductor equipment parts, and as a powder for solid lubrication and thermally conductive filler. Its properties vary widely between grades and pressing direction.

Applications

  • Nozzles and molds for molten metals
  • Semiconductor equipment parts
  • Solid lubrication

Fabrication

Machining

With ordinary tools, even HSS, like a hard plastic. The material is soft and chips at edges, so support well.

Moisture

Boric oxide bonded grades absorb moisture. Dry before hot use.

Strength

Relatively low. Not for load-bearing parts.

Common mistakes

Confusing it with CBN

hBN is soft; CBN is the second hardest material after diamond.

Using a boric oxide grade in humidity

It absorbs moisture and weakens.

Applying mechanical load

Strength is low.
