1144 Steel

A resulfurized medium-carbon steel with high manganese

Typical values for the temper in the title

1144 contains about 0.44% carbon, high manganese and sulfur. The sulfur breaks the chip, so it machines much better than 1045. Manganese and carbon give it high strength.

In the version known as Stressproof it is heavily cold drawn and stress relieved, reaching a very high yield strength without hardening, with good dimensional stability in machining. It is used for shafts and precise parts that need strength.

Conditions and heat treatment

1144 is usually chosen precisely to avoid heat treatment: the strength comes from cold work.

ConditionPropertiesWhen to choose it
Cold drawn and stress relieved (Stressproof)high yield without hardeningshafts and precise parts

Applications

  • Precision shafts and bars
  • High-strength machine parts
  • Piston rods
  • Automatic lathe parts
  • Pump shafts

Fabrication

Machining

Much better than 1045 thanks to the sulfur.

Dimensional stability

In the Stressproof version residual stresses are low, and the part warps less in machining.

Welding

Not recommended: sulfur causes cracking.

Common mistakes

Welding 1144

Sulfur causes cracking.

Replacing 1045 with 1144 in a shock-loaded part

Sulfur lowers transverse toughness.

Heat treating Stressproof without recalculating

Heat treatment removes the strength gained by cold work.
