17-4 PH (630) Stainless Steel

A Cr-Ni-Cu martensitic precipitation-hardening (PH) stainless steel

Typical values for the temper in the title

17-4 PH contains about 17% chromium, 4% nickel and 4% copper. It is supplied solution treated (Condition A), machined in that state, and then hardened by a simple treatment at a relatively low temperature (480–620 °C). During aging, tiny copper particles form and strengthen it.

The big advantage: very high strength (up to 1310 MPa and more) with better corrosion resistance than martensitic stainless, and the heat treatment hardly distorts the part, because it is done after machining and at low temperature. That makes it common in shafts, aerospace parts and pump and valve parts.

Conditions and heat treatment

Hardening has two steps: solution treatment at about 1040 °C (Condition A, the supplied state), then aging. The number after H is the aging temperature in Fahrenheit.

ConditionPropertiesWhen to choose it
Condition A (solution treated)medium strength, machinabledelivery condition: machine, then age
H900 (480 °C, 1 h)Rm min 1310 MPa, Rp0.2 min 1170 MPamaximum strength, low toughness
H1025 (550 °C, 4 h)Rm min 1070 MPa, Rp0.2 min 1000 MPaa balance of strength and toughness

Applications

  • Pump and propeller shafts
  • Aerospace and space parts
  • Valve parts and pump impellers
  • High-strength bolts
  • Molds and precision machine parts

Fabrication

Machining

Machine in Condition A or H1150. Heat treatment after machining causes almost no distortion, with a small shrinkage (on the order of tenths of a percent) that can be allowed for.

Welding

Welds well without preheat, usually with matching filler (ER630). After welding, age the part, and sometimes re-solution treat.

Corrosion

Better than martensitic stainless and close to 304 in many environments. In H900 it is more prone to stress corrosion cracking, so in such environments H1025 and above are chosen.

Common mistakes

Using H900 in a shock-loaded part or a corrosive environment

H900 is relatively brittle and prone to stress corrosion cracking. For such parts, H1025 to H1150.

Machining after aging to H900

The material is very hard. Machine in Condition A and age afterwards.

Forgetting to age after welding

The weld zone will be in a different condition from the part. Post-weld heat treatment is needed.
