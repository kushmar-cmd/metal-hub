🎯 Bearing Selection Guide
Bearing Selection Steps
6 Steps
1. LoadsFr (radial) · Fa (axial) · direction · dynamics
2. Speedn (RPM) · ndm ≤ 500K grease · 750K oil
3. Service LifeRequired L10h: 20K hours industrial · 50K+ critical
4. Dimensionsbore (d) ← shaft · OD (D) ← housing · B width
5. EnvironmentTemperature · humidity · contamination · vibration
6. Size SelectionC/P ≥ (L10h × 60n / 10⁶)^(1/p)
Equivalent Dynamic Load P
P = X·Fr + Y·Fa
X — Radial Factor1.0 if Fa/Fr ≤ e · 0.56 if Fa/Fr > e
Y — Axial Factor0 if Fa/Fr ≤ e · from catalog if > e
e — Contact Factor0.22-0.44 (depends on bearing and load)
6205 ExampleFr=5kN, Fa=1kN, e=0.30 → P=5×1+0=5kN
Static P0P0 = X0·Fr + Y0·Fa (for static load)
X, Y, e — from the manufacturer's catalog only!
Clearance — Internal Clearance
C2 · CN · C3 · C4 · C5
C2Small clearance — high precision, preload
CN (Normal)Default — most applications
C3Large clearance — high temp., press-fit mounting
C4Very large clearance — very high temp.
Rule of ThumbPress-fit mounting → C3. Temp. >80°C → C3
Too little clearance = overheating = failure. Too much = vibration and noise

P0-P2ISO 492

P6↑precision · general machinery, plummer block

Digit 2 — Diameter Series0=Extra Light · 1=Extra Light · 2=Light · 3=Medium · 4=Heavy
Digits 3-4 — bore00=10 · 01=12 · 02=15 · 03=17 · 04+=×5mm
SealsZ=metal shield · ZZ=×2 · RS=rubber · 2RS=×2 · RZ=low-friction seal

PrecisionP0(unmarked)/P6/P5/P4/P2
MaterialS=stainless · W=Stainless · X=special
Example: 6205-2RS-C3 = DG · series 2 · bore 25mm · rubber×2 · large clearance

📊 L10 Bearing Life Calculation
L10 — Basic Rating Life
ISO 28190% Survival
L10 (million revolutions)L10 = (C/P)^p
p — Exponent3 for ball · 10/3 for roller
L10h (hours)L10h = L10 × 10⁶ / (60 × n)
C — Dynamic LoadFrom manufacturer catalog (kN)
P — Equivalent LoadP = XFr + YFa
L10 = 90% of bearings will survive at least L10 hours
aISO Life — Modified Rating
ISO 281:2007More Accurate
Lnm = a1 × aISO × L10— modified life
a1 — Reliability Factor90%=1.0 · 95%=0.64 · 99%=0.21
aISO — Condition FactorLubrication + contamination + load
κ = ν/ν₁ν=actual viscosity · ν₁=required
κ≥2 + CleanlinessaISO up to 50! → L10m up to ×50

ndm — Speed Parameter
n × dm ≤ 500,000
dmdm = (bore + OD) / 2 — pitch diameter
ndm ≤ 500,000→ Grease suitable
ndm 500K-750K→ Special grease or oil
ndm > 750,000→ Oil mandatory
Example: 6205dm=(25+52)/2=38.5mm · n=12,000rpm · ndm=462K ← Grease ✅
⚠️ Bearing Failure Analysis
Fatigue Spalling
30% of All Failures
AppearanceFlakes on the raceway · ↑noise
CauseCyclic load → subsurface crack → spalling
Accelerating FactorsOverload · insufficient lubrication · contamination
PreventionC/P ≥ 3 · cleanliness · correct lubrication
Early DetectionVibration monitoring (SKF IMx) · Shock Pulse SPM
▶ YouTube
Brinelling
True / False Brinelling
True BrinellingStatic load exceeding P0 → permanent indentation
False BrinellingVibration during rest → fretting wear
AppearanceEvenly-spaced indentations at rolling element pitch
True PreventionC0/P0 ≥ 4 · upsize the bearing
False PreventionEP grease · SRB/TRB bearing type · avoid contact
▶ YouTube
Fluting
Electrical Current!
AppearanceParallel grooves on the raceway · dark discoloration
CauseVFD current passing through the bearing ← EDM discharges
VFD BearingVFD motors → high CMRV level
PreventionInsulated bearing (HV/H) · grounding brush · bonding
SKF HybridSi3N4 balls → electrical insulation!
Manufacturer: SKF Insocoat / Hybrid
▶ YouTube
Contamination
20% of All Failures
AppearanceDenting · accelerated wear · spalling
SourcesDust · debris · chips · water
ISO 4406Oil cleanliness: -/16/13 ← minimum
PreventionGood seals · oil filtration · proper storage
SPM Shock PulseContamination detection → dBsv > dBi + 20dB = problem
