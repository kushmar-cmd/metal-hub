Heat Treatment

An animated course on heat treatment: from what happens inside the steel on heating and cooling, through annealing, normalizing, hardening, quenching and tempering, surface hardening and heat treatment by material family, to furnaces and atmospheres, distortion and cracking, testing, specifications and costs. Each chapter explains what happens in the material, what the parameters are, what goes wrong and what drives the cost.
