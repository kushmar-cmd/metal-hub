MRR= Vc × fn × ap (cm³/min)

n (RPM)= 1000 × Vc / (π × Dc)
Vf= fz × z × n (mm/min)

tc= L / Vf (minutes)

FormulaVcTn = C → T = (C/Vc)^(1/n)
