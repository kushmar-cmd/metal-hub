Squeeze
Critical!
Application TypeSqueeze %Fill %Note
Static — internal15–25%65–80%Most common
Static — external15–25%65–80%Face seal
Dynamic axial10–20%55–70%Piston, rod
Dynamic rotary5–15%50–65%↓friction
Cryogenic20–30%70–85%Shrinkage in cold
High temperature20–25%70–85%Compression set
FormulaSqueeze% = (d₂ - h) / d₂ × 100
Fill%= π×d₂²/(4×h×w) × 100
▸ Too little squeeze = leakage. Too much = compression set = leakage!
▸ Always check Fill — the O-ring needs room to expand under pressure
▶ YouTube
Stretch
Ideal stretch1–5% — an O-ring slightly larger than the groove
Maximum6% — above this = ↓cord diameter = ↓squeeze!
CompressionUp to 3% (O-ring larger than the groove)
FormulaStretch% = (Groove ID − O-ring ID) / O-ring ID × 100
▸ A stretched O-ring = a smaller cord diameter = less squeeze!
▸ Choose an O-ring with an ID slightly smaller than the groove — 1-3% stretch

▸ Shore 70 = the default for most applications

Compression Set
DefinitionPercent of permanent set after sustained load — the O-ring doesn't return to its shape
FormulaCS% = (d₀ − d₁) / (d₀ − d_spacer) × 100
NBR 70CS ≈ 20% after 70°C/22h
FKMCS ≈ 15% after 175°C/22h
SiliconeCS ≈ 30–50% — relatively high!
PTFECS ≈ 60–70% — cold flow!
▸ High CS = the O-ring "stays flat" ← ↓squeeze ← leakage
▸ High temperature = ↑CS faster
▸ Kalrez CS = 5–15% — the best

O-Ring
Most CommonStatic+Dynamic
Max pressureStatic: 700 bar · Dynamic: 100 bar (no backup)
SpeedUp to 0.5 m/s axial · up to 2 m/s rotary
AdvantagesCheap, available, easy to install, bidirectional
DisadvantagesExtrusion at high pressure, spiral failure in dynamic use

X-Ring / Quad-Ring
Dynamic
Principle4 lips instead of 2 — ↓spiral failure in dynamic use
PressureUp to 400 bar
Advantage over O-ring↓friction, ↓torsion, ↑dynamic durability
DisadvantageMore expensive, less available
UseHydraulic piston, rod, reciprocating
Backup Ring
High Pressure
PrincipleA PTFE/nylon ring on the pressure side — prevents extrusion
Pressure with backupUp to 700 bar!
Single BackupUnidirectional pressure — backup on the pressure side
Double BackupBidirectional pressure — backup on both sides
MaterialPTFE (spiral-wound), nylon, polyester
▸ Extrusion gap: ≥70 bar → backup mandatory!
▸ Maximum gap: 0.05–0.1mm depending on Shore
U-Cup / Lip Seal
Piston/Rod
PrincipleA flexible lip pressed by pressure — self-energizing
PressureUp to 500 bar
SpeedUp to 1 m/s (polyurethane)
DirectionalityUnidirectional! Orient toward the pressure
MaterialPolyurethane, NBR, FKM, PTFE
UseHydraulic cylinders, pneumatic, actuators
▶ YouTube
Wiper / Scraper Seal
Rod Cleaning
PrincipleScrapes dirt off the rod as it enters the cylinder
PositionOutermost — the cylinder head
MaterialPolyurethane, NBR, PTFE
Failure without itDirt enters → scratches chrome plating → leakage!
▸ Wiper + rod seal = a complete rod protection set
▸ Always replace the wiper when replacing the rod seal
V-Ring Seal
Rotating Shaft
PrincipleA rubber ring pressed against the shaft shoulder — self-clamping
PressureLow — not for sealing under pressure
SpeedUp to 12 m/s
UseProtecting grease-lubricated bearings + dirt barrier
AdvantageNo groove needed! Installs directly on the shaft
ManufacturersSKF V-Ring, Freudenberg
PTFE Lip Seal / Rotary Shaft
High Speed
PrincipleA PTFE lip on a rotating shaft — ↓friction
SpeedUp to 20 m/s! (vs. NBR 6 m/s)
Temperature−200 to +260°C

Shaft RaRa 0.2–0.8μm — must pay attention!

ExtremeTemp+Pressure

🌀 Mechanical Face Seal
Operating Principle
Zero LeakagePumps
Principle2 lapped faces (primary/mating ring) pressed against each other — ↓leakage
Primary RingA ring rotating with the shaft (carbon/SiC/WC)
Mating RingA stationary ring in the pump housing (SiC/SS/ceramic)
SpringPresses the primary ring against the mating ring
Allowed leakage3–5 drops/hour — a mechanical seal isn't absolute zero leakage
Durability10,000–50,000 operating hours
▸ A thin liquid film between the faces = enables free rotation + ↓wear
▸ Never run dry! → immediate failure
▸ Flushing Plan (API 682) = cools + cleans the faces

Spiral Wound Gasket — Details
Very Common
StructureV-shaped metal strip + filler wound in a spiral
Inner RingMarked yellow — SS304/316
FillerGraphite (general) / PTFE (chemical)
WindingSS304 / SS316 / Inconel
Color codingGreen=graphite · white=PTFE · blue=mica
Seating StressMinimum: 69 MPa (ASME)
▸ Inner ring is mandatory! Prevents buckling under vacuum
▸ Storage: flat + dry — don't compress!
▶ YouTube
Ring Type Joint (RTJ)
High Pressure
PrincipleA soft metal ring in a groove — ← pressure clamps it tighter
Shape ROval — relatively low pressures
Shape RXPressure energized — ← self-clamping
Shape BXSuper high pressure — subsea, wellhead
MaterialMust be softer than the flange! Iron < CS < SS < Monel
▸ RTJ is single-use! Never reuse
▸ The groove must be clean and free of defects
▶ YouTube
