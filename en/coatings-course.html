Coatings, Finishes & Paint

An animated course on everything that happens to a part after machining: from surface preparation, through conversion coatings, metallic coatings and paint, to thermal spray, PVD and testing. Each chapter explains how the process works, what it gives you, how to design a part for it, what goes wrong, and what drives the cost.
