METAL HUB 🇮🇱

Smart Search & Concept Links

Bilingual search across 303 engineering materials and 1,000 terms, with equivalents in other standards, linked concepts and a learning trail. Type a material, a term or a standard designation (for example C45) and follow the links to learn.

How to use it (30 seconds)
  1. Type in the box, in English or Hebrew. A typo does not matter. Try the examples under the box.
  2. Click a result (a material or a term) to open its card.
  3. In the card open "Linked to...". Every button there is a link: an equivalent in another standard, a topic on the site, a term, a similar material or a video. Click a link to go to it, and you move from idea to idea and learn. Materials with a recorded cutting speed (for example 1018) also show a quick RPM calculator already fed with the material's data.
  4. The learning trail shows the path you took. Click a step to go back to it.
  5. Buttons with 🎬 or 🔗 open the video or the page on the site, in a new tab.
